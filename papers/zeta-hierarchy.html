The Minimal Balancing Multiplier and the Riemann Zeta Function at Positive Integers

Central Divisors, Power-Balanced Sets, Apéry’s Constant ζ(3), and r-Degree Zeta Unification

Dr. Hassan BALOUI — Mathematician & Independent Researcher (Ph.D., ENS Paris-Saclay)
⬇ Download Full-Text PDF ▶ Explore in Interactive Laboratory Zenodo DOI Record ↗

Abstract

Establishes that partitioning positive integers into quadratic and r-degree power shells links central conjugate divisors directly to the prime-counting function π(x) and positive integer values of the Riemann zeta function. Proves the sharp prime ceiling β(p) = ⌊√p⌋², the 4:1 intra-shell spectral gap, the ζ(3) asymptotic law for the square-multiplier set (OEIS A401528) and first moment of β(n) (OEIS A382286), and the r-degree parity split unifying ζ(2s+1) and ζ(2s).
